# Tornado routes and entrainment — 12 November 2026

Every weather cycle now generates a deterministic pair of cubic curves across the compiled Brisbane bounds. Entry/exit points, control points and CBD crossing vary with room seed and cycle. Opposite edges ensure map-scale coverage; the middle of the route always crosses the CBD while grounded. Position and velocity use the same route in the phone radar, visual storm, wind, damage and server validation. The 32-entry route cache is bounded. Storms remain forecast from the saved campaign clock or shared room clock; EF5 remains rare.

EF5 uses a 205 m core parameter, 470 m capture radius, 850 m inflow range, larger dense procedural wedge, stronger inflow and a wider temporary building-damage swath. Damage remains capped at 320 active building records and repairs automatically. The debris buffer remains 320 instances. Nearby traffic, including buses, can orbit in an EF5 until dissipation, then fall and recover their traffic routes. These are stylized gameplay values, not a calibrated tornado or structural simulation.

`VortexRide` owns entrained player motion. It ramps inward toward a rotating orbit, rises with bounded velocity, follows the moving vortex and releases when it weakens or ends. Mounted players keep their trolley; walking and driving stop applying competing motion during capture. Swept horizontal collisions, map bounds and upward box clearance remain active. Release applies downward/tangential velocity, gravity, ground recovery, an eight-second recapture cooldown and an eighteen-second recovery bound. Stability has a floor during capture. Zone changes and solo drill resets clear the state. Reloading does not preserve the exact orbit phase; existing airborne recovery handles the saved position.

All room clients derive the same tornado track. Actual player/trolley positions and mounted state use existing multiplayer replication. Server tests run a complete mounted orbit/release and confirm movement acceptance. Traffic debris and building animation remain local visual simulations; they are not networked rigid bodies.

Validation: native route sampling checks 120 distinct seeded paths, city crossing, bounds and continuity. Every EF tier is simulated through capture/orbit/release/recovery. `vortex-ride-browser.cjs` exercises the actual mapped Brisbane collision system on foot and mounted, rider alignment, release and landing, and renders the enlarged wedge. This is controlled browser automation, not a complete human campaign playthrough or a hardware performance benchmark.
